Austrian Grand Prix
AUT SPIELBERG, AUSTRIA · 4.318 KM
{{ s.v }}
{{ s.l }}

Nestled in the Styrian hills, Spielberg's 4.318 km circuit has become one of CGR League's most unpredictable battlegrounds across seven seasons. Max Verstappen is the only driver to win here twice — in Seasons 2 and 4 — yet even he couldn't string together true dominance. Ryan Reynolds delivered one of the circuit's signature moments with his Season 3 triumph, while brother Cole Reynolds went one better in Season 6 and leads all human drivers with 61 points at the track. Perhaps the most quietly impressive Spielberg performer is Giordano Temple, who despite never winning has racked up three podiums and boasts the best average finish of any human competitor at P7.8 — the model of relentless consistency. From Hamilton's opening-season victory to Perez's Season 5 upset, the Austrian Grand Prix has refused to crown a repeat champion in back-to-back years, making it the venue where form guides go to die.
