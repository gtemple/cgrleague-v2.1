Giordano Temple has worn seven different team colours across seven seasons and somehow made every garage feel like home — 832 points and 26 podiums across one of the most nomadic careers in CGR League history. Bahrain is where he truly comes alive: 102 points and two of his six career wins at a single circuit, a dominance that borders on obsession. His 16 Driver of the Day awards tell the real story — Temple doesn't just finish races, he makes them memorable. From Ferrari to Aston Martin the team changes, but the output stays remarkably consistent: an average finish inside the top nine, with flashes of brilliance that keep him perpetually dangerous.
