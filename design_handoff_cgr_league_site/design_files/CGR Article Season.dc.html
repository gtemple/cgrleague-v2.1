SEASON PREVIEW Season 7

F125 Arrives: 22 Rounds, 20 Drivers, and a Battle Nobody Can Predict

12 MARCH 2026 · 4 MIN READ

Can anyone dethrone Max Verstappen before he even gets comfortable? That's the question hanging over CGR League as Season 7 roars to life on a brand-new F125 platform — and the answer might depend less on raw pace and more on who can survive a grueling 22-round marathon that stretches from the floodlit asphalt of Bahrain all the way to the chaos of Interlagos.

This is the longest season in CGR League history by any measure that matters. Twenty-two rounds, including two sprint weekends in Austria and São Paulo, mean the points table will be a living, breathing document for months. The sprint format adds a fascinating wrinkle: those condensed weekends demand instant adaptability. There's no time to regroup between qualifying and the sprint — you either nail your setup or you hemorrhage points. In a title fight that could come down to single digits, the sprint rounds might end up being the most important entries on the entire calendar.

The grid itself is stacked. Red Bull fields Verstappen and Yuki Tsunoda, a pairing that blends relentless consistency with growing maturity. Ferrari welcomes Lewis Hamilton alongside Charles Leclerc — a partnership that looks sensational on paper but carries the weight of two massive egos and the eternal question of team orders. McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri present perhaps the most balanced lineup on the grid, and that internal tension could either fuel a constructors' charge or tear the team apart from within.

R. Reynolds steps into one of the most high-pressure seats on the grid: Mercedes. Paired with prodigious AI talent Kimi Antonelli, the Silver Arrows give him the machinery to compete at the very front — but every session will be scrutinized. The early flyaway rounds in Bahrain, Jeddah, and Imola will be critical for him to establish himself as the lead driver in that garage.

His brother, C. Reynolds, faces a very different challenge at Visa Cash App Racing Bulls — a midfield team with ambitions of punching above its weight. Paired with hungry young talent Isack Hadjar, the Racing Bulls garage could become a hotbed of intra-team rivalry. The sibling subplot between the Reynolds brothers adds an irresistible storyline: same bloodline, vastly different machinery, and bragging rights no trophy can replicate.

Giordano Temple arrives at Aston Martin with a point to prove. Slotting in alongside the ageless Fernando Alonso, Temple inherits a seat that demands consistency and racecraft in equal measure. Alonso is one of the most cerebral drivers on the grid, and Temple will have an extraordinary benchmark right across the garage. If he can run wheel-to-wheel with Alonso through Eau Rouge or hold his nerve through the Barcelona chicane, it will send a powerful message to the rest of the paddock.

Twenty-two rounds. Three human drivers carrying their teams' hopes. A regulation reset that could shuffle the established order. Season 7 has every ingredient for the most unpredictable campaign CGR League has ever seen. The lights go out in Bahrain — and after that, nobody knows what happens next.
