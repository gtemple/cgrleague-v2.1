Shanghai Showdown: C. Reynolds Looks to Extend Lead in Round 6

11 MARCH 2026 · 3 MIN READ

The CGR League circus rolls into Shanghai for Round 6 of Season 7, and the championship picture is beginning to sharpen. The 5.451-kilometer Shanghai International Circuit — with its punishing Turn 1-2-3 complex, long back straight, and technical final sector — has historically rewarded drivers who combine raw pace with patience. And patience, right now, might be exactly what separates the contenders from the pretenders.

C. Reynolds sits atop the standings with 90 points, a comfortable but far from insurmountable 17-point lead over Charles Leclerc. The Racing Bulls driver has been the model of consistency, and his Shanghai record tells a story of steady progression without the breakthrough win: P2 in Season 1, P5 in Season 2, P4 in Season 3, and P6 in Season 5. He's always been quick here but never dominant. A podium could be worth its weight in gold — he doesn't need to win, he needs to keep banking points and let the gap do the talking.

The history books scream one name at Shanghai: Max Verstappen. The Red Bull driver has won two of the four previous CGR League races held here, and he arrives in P3 with 58 points, trailing by 32. That's a significant deficit, but with 14 rounds still to come, Verstappen cannot be written off. A victory on what might be his strongest circuit could ignite a title charge.

Giordano Temple comes to Shanghai sitting P8 with 34 points, and this track holds special significance — he won here in Season 1, one of the most memorable victories of his CGR career. But his recent Shanghai form has been inconsistent. Temple is just one point behind teammate Fernando Alonso, making the intra-team battle at Aston Martin one of the most compelling subplots of the weekend.

R. Reynolds, meanwhile, sits dead last in P20 with zero points through five rounds. But Shanghai produced one of his best-ever results — a stunning P2 in Season 3. The volatility cuts both ways; he finished P20 here in Seasons 2 and 5 as well. Shanghai could be the circuit where R. Reynolds finally unlocks his season, or it could be another weekend of frustration.

HEAD TO HEAD
GT Giordano Temple VS
FA Fernando Alonso

Separated by just a single point in the championship, this intra-team Aston Martin duel could define their season. Temple won here in Season 1 but has been wildly inconsistent at Shanghai since, while Alonso's relentless consistency has kept him just ahead. Expect sparks when teammates with this much on the line go wheel-to-wheel.
